## Level 4: merges and balance history

Customers who consolidate their accounts get them merged, and auditors ask "what was this account's balance back then?". Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{merge_accounts}}(ts, keep, absorb) → bool` | Folds `absorb` into `keep`, then closes `absorb`. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if either account doesn't exist or they're the same account. |
| `{{balance_at}}(ts, id, at_ts) → balance or none` | The balance account `id` had right after everything that happened at or before `{{at_ts}}` (`{{at_ts}} <= ts`); *none* if no account with that id was open at `{{at_ts}}`. |

**What a merge moves.** `keep`'s balance grows by `absorb`'s balance, and its spending (for `{{top_spenders}}`) grows by `absorb`'s spending. Every **pending** payment of `absorb` now draws on `keep`, keeping its id and due time; from then on it can be cancelled only through `keep`. The merge itself isn't spending.

**After a merge** `absorb` no longer exists: every method treats it as an id that was never opened, and it's left out of the rankings. The id can be opened again later as a brand-new, empty account with no spending.

**History.** `{{balance_at}}` answers for whichever account held that id at `{{at_ts}}`: before a merge, an absorbed account's own balance; between the merge and a reopening, nothing. A scheduled payment changes the balance at its **due time**, even if the bank only noticed it at a later call.

### Example

```
(acc-a is open with 500, acc-b with 200, both deposited by time 4)
{{schedule_payment}}(5, "acc-b", 300, 15)     → "pay1"   (due at 20)
{{withdraw}}(6, "acc-b", 50)                  → 150
{{merge_accounts}}(10, "acc-a", "acc-b")      → true
{{balance}}(11, "acc-a")                      → 650
{{balance}}(12, "acc-b")                      → none
{{top_spenders}}(13, 3)                       → ["acc-a:50"]
{{balance}}(25, "acc-a")                      → 350      (pay1 ran at 20, from acc-a)
{{balance_at}}(26, "acc-b", 8)                → 150
{{balance_at}}(27, "acc-b", 10)               → none
{{balance_at}}(28, "acc-a", 19)               → 650
{{balance_at}}(29, "acc-a", 20)               → 350
```

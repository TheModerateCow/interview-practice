## Level 2: rankings

The bank's fraud and marketing teams want to see who moves the most money out, and who holds the most. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{top_spenders}}(ts, n) → list of string` | Up to `n` open accounts as `"id:spent"`, most spent first; ties by id ascending. An account's **spending** is the total of its successful withdrawals plus the transfers it **sent**. Deposits and transfers received don't count, and neither does anything that failed. Accounts that spent nothing are included (with `0`) if there's room. |
| `{{accounts_above}}(ts, min) → list of string` | Every open account whose balance is **at least** `min`, as `"id:balance"`, largest balance first; ties by id ascending. |

Ids sort in plain string order (by code unit, so `"ACC-A"` comes before `"acc-a"`). Return an empty list when nothing matches (or `n` is 0).

### Example

```
(acc-a, acc-b and acc-c are open, each with 1000 deposited)
{{withdraw}}(7, "acc-a", 300)                 → 700
{{transfer}}(8, "acc-c", "acc-a", 300)        → 700
{{withdraw}}(9, "acc-b", 50)                  → 950
{{top_spenders}}(10, 2)                       → ["acc-a:300", "acc-c:300"]
{{top_spenders}}(11, 5)                       → ["acc-a:300", "acc-c:300", "acc-b:50"]
{{accounts_above}}(12, 950)                   → ["acc-a:1000", "acc-b:950"]
```

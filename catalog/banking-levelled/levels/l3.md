## Level 3: scheduled payments

Customers can book a bill payment to go out later. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{schedule_payment}}(ts, id, amount, delay) → payment id or none` | Books a payment of `amount` out of the account, due at `ts + delay` (`delay` is always positive). Returns the payment's id: `"pay1"`, `"pay2"`, `"pay3"`, … numbered across the whole bank. Returns *none* if the account doesn't exist. |
| `{{cancel_payment}}(ts, id, payment_id) → bool` | Cancels a payment that hasn't run yet and draws on account `id`. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the payment doesn't exist, has already run (paid or failed), was cancelled, or draws on another account. |
| `{{payment_status}}(ts, payment_id) → string` | `"pending"`, `"paid"`, `"failed"` or `"cancelled"`; `""` for an id that was never issued. |

**When a payment runs.** A payment due at time `t` runs **at `t`**, before anything else that happens at `t`: a call with timestamp `t` already sees it. The bank has no clock of its own: due payments are settled by the next call of **any** method whose timestamp is at or after their due time. When several payments are due by then, they run in order of due time; payments due at the same time run in the order they were scheduled.

- If the account holds at least `amount`, the payment is taken out (status `"paid"`) and counts toward the account's **spending** in `{{top_spenders}}`.
- Otherwise it takes nothing and its status becomes `"failed"`. A failed payment is never retried.

### Example

```
(acc-a is open, with 300 deposited)
{{schedule_payment}}(10, "acc-a", 200, 10)    → "pay1"   (due at 20)
{{schedule_payment}}(11, "acc-a", 150, 4)     → "pay2"   (due at 15)
{{balance}}(14, "acc-a")                      → 300
{{balance}}(15, "acc-a")                      → 150      (pay2 ran at 15)
{{deposit}}(20, "acc-a", 50)                  → 200      (pay1 ran first, and failed)
{{payment_status}}(21, "pay1")                → "failed"
{{cancel_payment}}(22, "acc-a", "pay2")       → false    (already paid)
```

# Phase 3: settlement at scale

Settlement is live, and the settlement worker now polls every merchant's `settle` every couple of seconds so funds become available as soon as their hold ends. For small merchants that's fine. For the largest merchant, each poll gets slower as their history grows. After a few weeks of traffic the worker can't finish one round of polls before the next one is due.

The workload to handle: one merchant, **15,000 captures** a minute apart (a mix of `card` and `bank`), with **8 `settle` polls** between each capture and the next. The whole run, captures included, must take **under 1 second**. Settlement results (batches, charges, balances) must not change.

Find where the time goes and fix it. Remember that card and bank charges have different holds, so a charge captured later can become available sooner.

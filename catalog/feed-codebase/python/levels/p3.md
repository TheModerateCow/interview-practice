## Phase 3: morning peak

The region's load test: 2,000 accounts that have each posted once a day for a month (60,000 posts), and 40 heavy readers, each following 150 accounts, who open the app at the same moment. Each reader's first `home_feed` page takes about a tenth of a second, and their `catch_up` carousel just as long, so the 40 together take several seconds. The app server's budget is half a second for all 40 feeds, and the same for all 40 catch-ups.

Find the bottleneck and fix it. Behaviour must not change: every phase 1 and phase 2 test must still pass, and every feed must hold the same posts in the same order.

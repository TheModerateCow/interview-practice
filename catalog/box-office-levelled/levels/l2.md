## Level 2: rankings

The promoter wants to see who is buying and what is selling. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{top_buyers}}(ts, event_id, n) → list of strings` | Up to `n` buyers of `event_id` as `"buyer(qty)"`: most tickets first, ties by buyer name ascending. Only buyers who currently own at least one ticket. An empty list if the event doesn't exist or nobody owns a ticket. |
| `{{busiest_events}}(ts, n) → list of strings` | Up to `n` events as `"event_id(sold)"`, where `sold` is the number of tickets currently owned for it: most sold first, ties by event id ascending. Events with nothing sold are included. |

Refunds count: a refunded ticket is no longer owned or sold.

### Example

```
{{create_event}}(1, "jazz-night", 10); {{create_event}}(2, "folk-fest", 10)
{{buy}}(3, "jazz-night", "maya", 2); {{buy}}(4, "jazz-night", "leo", 2); {{buy}}(5, "folk-fest", "sam", 5)
{{top_buyers}}(6, "jazz-night", 5) → ["leo(2)", "maya(2)"]
{{busiest_events}}(7, 5)           → ["folk-fest(5)", "jazz-night(4)"]
```

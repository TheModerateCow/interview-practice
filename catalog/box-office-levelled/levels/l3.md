## Level 3: checkout holds

At checkout, seats are held for a buyer for a short time while they pay. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{hold}}(ts, event_id, buyer, qty, ttl) → string` | Holds `qty` seats of `event_id` for `buyer` and returns the hold's id. Ids are `"h1"`, `"h2"`, `"h3"`, … in order, counted across the whole office; a failed call uses no id. Returns `""`, changing nothing, if the event doesn't exist, `qty <= 0`, `ttl <= 0`, or fewer than `qty` seats are available. |
| `{{confirm}}(ts, hold_id) → bool` | The buyer paid: the held seats become tickets the buyer owns. Returns `false` if the hold doesn't exist, was already confirmed, or has expired. |

A hold made at time `t` with `ttl` expires at `t + ttl`: from that timestamp on it holds nothing and can't be confirmed.

While a hold is live, its seats are **not available**: `{{available}}`, `{{buy}}` and other holds can't use them. Held seats are **not owned or sold** until confirmed, so they don't appear in `{{top_buyers}}` or `{{busiest_events}}`. Confirmed tickets behave exactly like bought ones (they can be refunded).

### Example

```
{{create_event}}(1, "jazz-night", 10)
{{hold}}(10, "jazz-night", "maya", 4, 5)  → "h1"
{{available}}(12, "jazz-night")           → 6
{{confirm}}(15, "h1")                     → false   (expired at 15)
{{available}}(15, "jazz-night")           → 10
```

## Level 4: limit orders on both sides

The book becomes a full two-sided market. Traders can now send **buy** orders that rest too, on the **bid** side, and any order can trade the moment it arrives. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{submit}}(order_id, side, price, qty) → shares or none` | A limit order: `side` is `"buy"` or `"sell"`. It first trades against the other side of the book as far as its price allows, then whatever is left of it rests at `price` on its own side (at the back of that price's queue). Returns the number of shares it traded on arrival. Returns *none*, changing nothing, if `order_id` has been used before. |
| `{{best_bid}}() → price or none` | The highest price at which any buy order rests; *none* if there's none. |
| `{{order_status}}(order_id) → string` | `"state:filled"`: the state is `open` (resting, even if partly traded), `filled` or `cancelled`, and `filled` is the number of shares the order has traded so far. `""` for an id that was never used. |

**Matching.** A buy trades with resting sells priced **at or below** its price, cheapest first; a sell trades with resting buys priced **at or above** its price, highest first. Within a price, orders trade in queue order. Every trade happens at the **resting** order's price. Resting orders that run out of shares are filled and leave the book, as in level 3; the arriving order is filled if nothing of it is left, and then it never rests.

**What else changes.**
- `{{add_order}}(order_id, price, qty)` is now a sell `{{submit}}`: it trades with resting buys first (still returning `true`/`false`).
- `{{cancel_order}}`, `{{amend_order}}`, `{{depth_at}}` and `{{queue_at}}` work on both sides. A price never holds both buys and sells, since they would have traded.
- `{{best_ask}}`, `{{top_levels}}`, `{{quote_buy}}` and `{{market_buy}}` still look only at the ask side.

A busy session has thousands of resting orders on each side and orders that cross the book all the time.

### Example

```
(s1 101x5 and s2 102x5 are resting sells)
{{submit}}("b1", "buy", 100, 4)    → 0       (nothing at or below 100: b1 rests)
{{best_bid}}()                     → 100
{{submit}}("b2", "buy", 102, 7)    → 7       (5 from s1 at 101, 2 from s2 at 102)
{{order_status}}("s1")             → "filled:5"
{{order_status}}("s2")             → "open:2"
{{submit}}("s3", "sell", 99, 6)    → 4       (all of b1 at 100; 2 shares rest at 99)
{{best_ask}}()                     → 99
{{order_status}}("b1")             → "filled:4"
{{cancel_order}}("s3")             → true
{{order_status}}("s3")             → "cancelled:4"
{{order_status}}("zz")             → ""
{{submit}}("b2", "buy", 1, 1)      → none
```

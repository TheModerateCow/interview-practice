## Phase 3: a busy day

The platform's biggest customer runs integrations that call the gateway every second, all day. By the afternoon each `allow` on their keys takes milliseconds instead of microseconds, and it gets worse every hour; the hourly billing reports for their org slow down the same way. A day's traffic for two keys (about 40,000 calls) has to go through in well under a second and a half.

Find the bottleneck and fix it. Behaviour must not change: every phase 1 and phase 2 test must still pass, and every call must get the same decision.

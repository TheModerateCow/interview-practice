## Level 3: compliance report

For the monthly SLO review, each service reports its best run: the longest stretch of **consecutive** minutes during which it stayed within budget. Minutes are pooled, not judged one by one: a noisy minute with little traffic can sit inside a stretch that is within budget overall. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{longest_within_budget}}(service, start, end) → string or none` | The longest stretch of consecutive minutes inside `start..end` that is **within budget**, as `"first-last"` (both minutes inclusive). When several stretches share the longest length, the one that starts earliest. `""` if no single minute in the range is within budget. *None* if the service doesn't exist or has no budget. |

**Within budget** means `errors * 1 000 000 <= requests * budget_ppm` for the stretch's pooled totals, so a stretch exactly on budget counts. Minutes with no traffic count like any other minute: they add nothing to either total, and a stretch with no requests at all is within budget. The range may run past the latest recorded minute; those minutes have no traffic.

Services keep months of minutes, and the review asks about long ranges: a range can span tens of thousands of minutes.

### Example

```
(api has budget 20000 (2%) and minutes 0-5: requests [120, 80, 200, 40, 160, 100], errors [2, 6, 1, 5, 0, 9])
{{longest_within_budget}}("api", 0, 5)               → "2-4"    (6 errors in 400 requests, though minute 3 alone runs at 12.5%)
{{longest_within_budget}}("api", 0, 2)               → "0-0"    (minutes 0-2 hold 9 in 400; minutes 0 and 2 tie, 0 is earlier)
{{longest_within_budget}}("api", 4, 9)               → "6-9"    (minutes 6-9 have no traffic)
{{longest_within_budget}}("api", 5, 5)               → ""
{{longest_within_budget}}("web", 0, 5)               → none
```

## Level 3: scheduled payments

Customers can book a bill payment to go out later. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `SchedulePayment(ts int, id string, amount, delay int) (string, bool)` | Books a payment of `amount` out of the account, due at `ts + delay` (`delay` is always positive). Returns the payment's id and `true`: `"pay1"`, `"pay2"`, `"pay3"`, … numbered across the whole bank. Returns `"", false` if the account doesn't exist. |
| `CancelPayment(ts int, id, paymentID string) bool` | Cancels a payment that hasn't run yet and draws on account `id`. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the payment doesn't exist, has already run (paid or failed), was cancelled, or draws on another account. |
| `PaymentStatus(ts int, paymentID string) string` | `"pending"`, `"paid"`, `"failed"` or `"cancelled"`; `""` for an id that was never issued. |

**When a payment runs.** A payment due at time `t` runs **at `t`**, before anything else that happens at `t`: a call with timestamp `t` already sees it. The bank has no clock of its own: due payments are settled by the next call of **any** method whose timestamp is at or after their due time. When several payments are due by then, they run in order of due time; payments due at the same time run in the order they were scheduled.

- If the account holds at least `amount`, the payment is taken out (status `"paid"`) and counts toward the account's **spending** in `TopSpenders`.
- Otherwise it takes nothing and its status becomes `"failed"`. A failed payment is never retried.

### Example

```
(acc-a is open, with 300 deposited)
SchedulePayment(10, "acc-a", 200, 10)      -> "pay1", true    (due at 20)
SchedulePayment(11, "acc-a", 150, 4)       -> "pay2", true    (due at 15)
Balance(14, "acc-a")                       -> 300, true
Balance(15, "acc-a")                       -> 150, true       (pay2 ran at 15)
Deposit(20, "acc-a", 50)                   -> 200, true       (pay1 ran first, and failed)
PaymentStatus(21, "pay1")                  -> "failed"
CancelPayment(22, "acc-a", "pay2")         -> false           (already paid)
```

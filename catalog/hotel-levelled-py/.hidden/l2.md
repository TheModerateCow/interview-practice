## Level 2: searching and ranking

The front desk wants to find free rooms and see who stays most. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `available_rooms(day, room_type: str, check_in: int, check_out: int) -> list[str]` | The ids of every room of `room_type` that `book` could book for those nights: cheapest nightly rate first, ties by room id ascending. An empty list if `check_in >= check_out` or no room fits. |
| `top_guests(day, n: int) -> list[str]` | Up to `n` guests as `"guest(nights)"`, where `nights` is the total number of nights across all their bookings, in any room: most nights first, ties by guest name ascending. Cancelled bookings don't count; guests with no nights left out. |

### Example

```
add_room(1, "101", "double", 12000); add_room(1, "102", "double", 10000); add_room(1, "103", "double", 10000)
book(2, "maya", "103", 10, 12)
available_rooms(3, "double", 11, 14)  -> ["102", "101"]
book(4, "leo", "101", 20, 23)
top_guests(5, 5)                      -> ["leo(3)", "maya(2)"]
```

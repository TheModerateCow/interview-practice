## Level 4: the electric van

The fleet now includes an electric van. Every road drains some of its battery, some junctions have a charger, and the van can't drive a road it doesn't have the charge for. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{add_charger}}(ts, junction, rate) → bool` | Installs a charger at the junction that adds **one** unit to the battery every `rate` minutes (`rate >= 1`). Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the junction doesn't exist or already has a charger. |
| `{{set_energy}}(ts, road, units) → bool` | Driving the road (either way) now uses `units` of battery (`units >= 0`). Every road uses `0` until this is called. Returns `false` if the road doesn't exist. |
| `{{fastest_delivery}}({{ts}}, {{origin}}, {{dest}}, {{capacity}}, {{charge}}) → int` | The fewest minutes for the van to get from `{{origin}}` to `{{dest}}` along open roads, with a battery that holds at most `{{capacity}}` units and starts with `{{charge}}` (`0 <= {{charge}} <= {{capacity}} <= 100`). `0` if they're the same junction; `-1` if either junction doesn't exist or the van can't get there. |

**Battery rules.** The van can drive a road only if it has at least the road's units; it may arrive with exactly `0`. At a junction with a charger it may stop and add any whole number of units, as long as the battery stays within `{{capacity}}`, paying `rate` minutes per unit. It may stop at any charger any number of times, including on the way back past one.

### Example

```
(junctions depot, j1, j2, j3, cust; roads, as minutes and units:
 r1 depot-j1 10/2, r2 j1-j2 10/2, r3 j2-cust 10/6, r4 depot-j3 5/4, r5 j3-cust 5/6)
{{add_charger}}(16, "j1", 5)                  → true
{{add_charger}}(17, "j2", 1)                  → true
{{add_charger}}(18, "j2", 3)                  → false
{{add_charger}}(19, "nowhere", 1)             → false
{{fastest_route}}(20, "depot", "cust")        → 10
{{fastest_delivery}}(21, "depot", "cust", 10, 4)  → 36
{{fastest_delivery}}(22, "depot", "cust", 10, 10) → 10
{{set_energy}}(23, "r4", 5)                   → true
{{fastest_delivery}}(24, "depot", "cust", 10, 10) → 30
{{set_energy}}(25, "ghost", 1)                → false
```

With 4 units, depot-j3-cust needs 10 and j3 has no charger. The van drives depot-j1-j2 (20 min, arriving empty), adds 6 units at j2's cheap charger (6 min) and drives on to cust (10 min): 36. Charging at j1 costs more, and filling up at j2 would waste 4 minutes.

## Level 4: arrivals, no-shows and repeat offenders

The host now seats parties as they arrive, the book stops holding tables for parties who never come, and guests who keep not turning up lose the right to book ahead. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{seat}}(ts, reservation_id) → bool` | The party has arrived and sits down. Returns `false` if the reservation doesn't exist, is cancelled, is already seated, or is a no-show. |
| `{{no_shows}}(ts, name) → int` | How many of `name`'s reservations have become no-shows by `ts`. |

**No-shows.** A table is held for 15 minutes past its slot. A reservation that isn't seated by `slot + 15` is a no-show: from `ts >= slot + 15` on it behaves like a cancelled reservation. `{{table_of}}` returns `""`, its tables show as free in `{{party_at}}` and `{{free_tables}}`, and `{{cancel}}` and `{{seat}}` return `false`. A seated party keeps its tables.

New rules for what you already have:

- **Too late to book.** `{{book}}` returns `""` and `{{join_waitlist}}` returns `false` once the slot's grace period is over (`ts >= slot + 15`). Before that, booking for a slot that has already started is fine.
- **Seated parties** can't be cancelled.
- **Repeat no-shows.** A guest with 2 or more no-shows can't `{{book}}` until 1440 minutes (a day) after their latest no-show, which happened at its `slot + 15`. Cancelled and seated reservations never count. The ban covers booking ahead only: a banned guest can still `{{join_waitlist}}` and be seated from the waitlist.

### Example

```
{{add_table}}(1, "T1", 4)
{{book}}(2, "maya", 2, 1140)            → "r1"
{{seat}}(1150, "r1")                    → true
{{book}}(1151, "leo", 2, 1260)          → "r2"
{{table_of}}(1274, "r2")                → "T1"
{{table_of}}(1275, "r2")                → ""      (not seated by 1260 + 15)
{{book}}(1276, "leo", 2, 2580)          → "r3"
{{seat}}(2595, "r3")                    → false   (a no-show from 2595)
{{no_shows}}(2596, "leo")               → 2
{{book}}(2600, "leo", 2, 4020)          → ""      (banned until 2595 + 1440 = 4035)
{{join_waitlist}}(2601, "leo", 2, 2700) → true    (walk-ins are fine; T1 is free)
```

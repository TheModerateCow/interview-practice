## Level 4: meetings that need a quorum

Not every meeting needs everyone: a design review might need any 3 of the 5 reviewers. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{earliest_quorum_meeting}}({{ids}}, {{quorum}}, {{duration}}, {{from}}, {{to}}) → int` | The earliest UTC minute `t` with `{{from}} <= t` and `t + {{duration}} <= {{to}}` such that at least `{{quorum}}` of the attendees in `{{ids}}` are each free for the **whole** of `[t, t + {{duration}})`. `-1` if there is no such `t`, or if any id is unknown. |
| `{{free_for}}({{ids}}, {{start}}, {{duration}}) → list of string` | The attendees in `{{ids}}` who are free for the whole of `[{{start}}, {{start}} + {{duration}})`, sorted by id (plain string order). Unknown ids are left out. |

- `{{quorum}} >= 1`, and it may be larger than the number of ids (then the answer is `-1`). Other inputs follow the level 3 rules.
- The quorum must be the **same** people for the whole meeting: two attendees who are each free for half of it, one after the other, count as nobody.
- Searches over a large calendar must stay as fast as in level 3.

### Example

```
(ana: offset 0,    hours 540-1020,                busy 540-610 and 660-720)
(ben: offset 60,   hours 540-1020 = UTC 480-960,  busy 500-630)
(cho: offset -120, hours 480-720  = UTC 600-840,  no busy blocks)
{{earliest_quorum_meeting}}(["ana", "ben", "cho"], 2, 60, 0, 1440)   → 630
{{earliest_quorum_meeting}}(["ana", "ben", "cho"], 3, 60, 0, 1440)   → 720
{{earliest_quorum_meeting}}(["ana", "ben", "cho"], 4, 60, 0, 1440)   → -1
{{free_for}}(["cho", "ana", "ben"], 630, 60)                         → ["ben", "cho"]
```

At 630 ana is free for only 30 minutes before her 660 block, but ben and cho can both hold the full hour.

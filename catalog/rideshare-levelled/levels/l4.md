## Level 4: planning the radius

Before a surge, operations wants to know how wide the radius would have to be to serve a given number of the riders waiting now. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{min_radius}}(ts, need) → int` | The smallest radius `R >= 0` at which `{{max_served}}` would be at least `need`, with the drivers and riders as they are now. It doesn't change the radius that's set. |

- If `need <= 0`, return `0` (even with nobody on the corridor).
- If fewer than `need` riders are waiting, or fewer than `need` drivers are idle, no radius is enough: return `-1`. Drivers on a trip don't count.

The corridor can hold thousands of drivers and riders spread over the whole million kilometres, so the answer can be a very large radius, and it has to come back fast.

### Example

```
(d1 idle at 4, d2 at 10, d3 at 30; r1 waiting at 2, r2 at 9, r3 at 15)
{{set_radius}}(7, 1)
{{min_radius}}(8, 2)                          → 2        (r1 with d1, r2 with d2)
{{min_radius}}(9, 3)                          → 15       (r3 has to reach d3)
{{min_radius}}(10, 4)                         → -1       (only 3 riders)
{{min_radius}}(11, 0)                         → 0
{{max_served}}(12)                            → 1        (the radius is still 1)
```

## Level 4: warranty limits

Battery makers void the warranty if a battery cycles too hard, so customers can cap how much energy it sends out each day. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{set_discharge_limit}}(id, kwh) → bool` | From now on the battery may discharge at most `{{kwh}}` kWh (0 or more) per day. Setting it again replaces the old limit. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the battery isn't registered. |

**What the limit changes.**

- `{{discharge}}` also returns *none*, changing nothing, once the battery has already discharged `{{kwh}}` kWh **on the day it names**. Every discharge recorded on that day counts, including ones recorded before the limit was set. Each new day starts again from 0.
- `{{best_profit}}` plans within the limit: the schedule discharges at most `{{kwh}}` kWh over the day. Charging isn't limited.

A battery with no limit set behaves as before. Full-length days with large batteries and limits must still be answered quickly.

### Example

```
(home-1 is registered with capacity 2 and wear 3; day 1's tariff is [12, 9, 25, 14, 30, 8, 11, 27])
{{set_discharge_limit}}("home-1", 2)          → true
{{best_profit}}("home-1", 1)                  → 34       (charge at 9 and 8, discharge at 30 and 27)
{{set_discharge_limit}}("home-1", 1)          → true
{{best_profit}}("home-1", 1)                  → 18       (charge at 9, discharge at 30)
{{charge}}("home-1", 1, 0)                    → 1
{{discharge}}("home-1", 1, 2)                 → 0
{{charge}}("home-1", 1, 3)                    → 1
{{discharge}}("home-1", 1, 4)                 → none     (1 kWh already sent out on day 1)
{{set_discharge_limit}}("home-7", 1)          → false
```

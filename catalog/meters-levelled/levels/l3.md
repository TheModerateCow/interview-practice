## Level 3: the best schedule

Customers ask "how much could my battery have made today?". The planner answers with the best schedule the battery could run over a day's tariff. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{best_profit}}(id, day) → pence or none` | The largest total a schedule over that day's tariff could earn for this battery. *None* if the battery isn't registered or the day has no tariff. |

**The schedule** is a what-if: it ignores what the battery has actually recorded and changes nothing. It follows the Level 1 rules for this battery:

- it starts the day **empty** and never holds more than the battery's capacity;
- in each slot it does exactly one thing: charge 1 kWh (paying the price), discharge 1 kWh (earning the price minus wear), or stay idle;
- energy still stored at the end of the day is worth nothing.

Idling all day earns `0`, so the answer is never negative. A day can have up to 50 slots, and the planner must answer quickly for full-length days: trying every schedule takes far too long.

### Example

```
(home-1 is registered with capacity 2 and wear 3)
{{publish_tariff}}(1, [12, 9, 25, 14, 30, 8, 11, 27])   → true
{{best_profit}}("home-1", 1)                  → 44
{{best_profit}}("home-1", 2)                  → none     (no tariff for day 2)
{{best_profit}}("home-9", 1)                  → none
```

The best schedule charges at 12 and 9, discharges at 25, idles at 14, discharges at 30, charges at 8, idles at 11 and discharges at 27: `-12 - 9 + (25-3) + (30-3) - 8 + (27-3) = 44`.

## Level 2: pricing and late returns

Customers now pay when they bring a car back, and the desk wants to chase late ones. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{charges}}({{ts}}, {{customer}})` | The total, in cents, `{{customer}}` has been charged for all the cars they have returned. `0` for a customer with none (a car still out isn't charged yet). |
| `{{overdue}}({{ts}})` | The cars still out past their due time, as `"car_id(customer)"`: earliest due time first, ties by car id ascending. An empty list if none. |

**Due time.** A car rented at `{{ts}}` for `{{days}}` days is due back at `{{ts}} + 24 × {{days}}`. It is overdue only once the time is **after** that: a car due at 34 is not overdue at 34, and is at 35.

**Rates** per day, in cents: `economy` 3000, `compact` 4500, `suv` 7000.

**The charge** is made when the car is returned:

- The car's rate for every **started** day it was out, counted in hours from the rental: 1 to 24 hours is 1 day, 25 hours is 2. Returning in the same hour still costs 1 day. Returning early costs only the days used.
- **Late days cost double:** each started day past the due time adds one more day's rate on top.

### Example

```
{{add_car}}(1, "c1", "compact", "downtown"); {{add_car}}(1, "c2", "suv", "downtown")
{{rent}}(8, "maya", "downtown", "compact", 2)  -> "c1"   (due at 56)
{{rent}}(10, "leo", "downtown", "suv", 1)      -> "c2"   (due at 34)
{{overdue}}(34)                                -> []
{{overdue}}(35)                                -> ["c2(leo)"]
{{return_car}}(40, "c2")                       -> true
{{charges}}(41, "leo")                         -> 21000  (30 hours: 2 days, 6 hours late: 1 more, 3 x 7000)
{{return_car}}(56, "c1")                       -> true
{{charges}}(57, "maya")                        -> 9000   (48 hours, on time: 2 x 4500)
```

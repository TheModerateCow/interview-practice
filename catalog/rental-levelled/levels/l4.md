## Level 4: reservations

Customers can now reserve a class of car at a branch for a future period, and walk-in rentals mustn't take the cars those reservations need. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{reserve}}({{ts}}, {{customer}}, {{branch}}, {{car_class}}, {{start}}, {{days}})` | Reserves a car of `{{car_class}}` at `{{branch}}` from hour `{{start}}` to `{{start}} + 24 × {{days}}` and returns the reservation's id: `"r1"`, `"r2"`, … in order; a failed call uses no id. Returns `""` if `{{car_class}}` is invalid, `{{days}} <= 0`, `{{start}} < {{ts}}`, or the branch is full for that period (below). |
| `{{pick_up}}({{ts}}, {{reservation_id}})` | The customer collects the reserved car: the lowest-id car of that class parked at the branch, rented to them and due back at the reservation's end. Returns the car id, or `""` if the reservation doesn't exist or was already picked up, `{{ts}}` is outside its period (before `{{start}}`, or at or after its end), the customer already has a car out, or no car of that class is parked there. |

A reservation is **pending** until it is picked up or its period ends without a pick-up. Two periods overlap if each starts before the other ends: a reservation ending at 54 doesn't overlap one starting at 54.

- **Full branch.** `{{reserve}}` fails if the pending reservations for the same branch and class that overlap the new period already number as many as the cars of that class belonging to the branch (parked there, or rented out from there).
- **Holds.** A walk-in `{{rent}}` covers `{{ts}}` to its due time. It succeeds only if the parked cars of that class at the branch outnumber the pending reservations for that branch and class that overlap its period; otherwise it returns `""`.
- A picked-up rental is charged like any other: from the pick-up time, late after the reservation's end, with a drop fee if it's dropped elsewhere. `{{available}}` still counts every parked car.

### Example

```
{{add_car}}(1, "c1", "suv", "airport"); {{add_car}}(1, "c2", "suv", "airport")
{{reserve}}(2, "maya", "airport", "suv", 48, 2)  -> "r1"   (hours 48 to 96)
{{rent}}(3, "leo", "airport", "suv", 3)          -> "c1"
{{rent}}(4, "ana", "airport", "suv", 2)          -> ""     (4 to 52 overlaps r1: c2 is held for maya)
{{rent}}(5, "ana", "airport", "suv", 1)          -> "c2"   (5 to 29 doesn't)
{{return_car}}(28, "c2")                         -> true
{{pick_up}}(50, "r1")                            -> "c2"
{{status}}(51, "c2")                             -> "rented:maya"
```

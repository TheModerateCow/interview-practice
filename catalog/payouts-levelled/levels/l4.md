## Level 4: reserves and chargebacks

Risk wants to hold back part of a merchant's money for a while, and card networks send chargebacks. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{set_reserve}}(day, {{merchant_id}}, {{reserve_bps}}, {{hold_days}})` | From now on, part of each new sale of the merchant is held in a rolling reserve (rules below). `{{reserve_bps}} = 0` turns the reserve off. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the merchant doesn't exist, `{{reserve_bps}}` is outside `0..10000`, or `{{hold_days}} < 1`. |
| `{{reserve_held}}(day, {{merchant_id}})` | The total reserve of the merchant's sales that hasn't been released yet. `0` for an unknown merchant. |
| `{{chargeback}}(day, {{sale_id}})` | The buyer's bank reverses the sale. The merchant is debited what's left of the sale (its amount minus everything refunded) **plus a 1500 chargeback fee**; returns that debit. The platform keeps its fee. Returns `-1`, changing nothing, if the sale doesn't exist, was already charged back, or was fully refunded. |

**The reserve.** A sale's reserve is `net × {{reserve_bps}} / 10000`, rounded half up like the fee, using the merchant's setting when the sale is recorded (sales recorded earlier keep theirs). The reserve settles on day `d + {{hold_days}}` instead of `d + 3`; the rest of the net settles on `d + 3` as before. `{{balance}}` includes held reserve; `{{available}}` doesn't until it's released. A refund or chargeback doesn't change when a sale's reserve is released.

**Chargebacks** count at once, like refunds: they can take the balance below 0, and later settled money covers it before anything is paid out. `{{activity}}` lists them as `"day:chargeback:-amount"`. After a chargeback, `{{refund}}` on that sale returns `-1`.

### Example

```
add_merchant(1, "acme", 290)
set_reserve(1, "acme", 1000, 10)   -> true
record_sale(2, "acme", 10000)      -> "s1"   (net 9710: 8739 settles day 5, reserve 971 day 12)
record_sale(3, "acme", 2000)       -> "s2"   (net 1942: 1748 settles day 6, reserve 194 day 13)
reserve_held(4, "acme")            -> 1165
chargeback(4, "s2")                -> 3500   (2000 + 1500)
available(5, "acme")               -> 5239
run_payouts(5)                     -> ["acme:5239"]
balance(5, "acme")                 -> 2913
reserve_held(12, "acme")           -> 194
run_payouts(13)                    -> ["acme:2913"]
```

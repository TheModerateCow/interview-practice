## Level 3: settlement and payouts

The platform now pays merchants out, but only money from sales that have **settled**. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{available}}(day, {{merchant_id}})` | What a payout would pay the merchant today (rules below). Can be negative. `0` for an unknown merchant. |
| `{{run_payouts}}(day)` | Pays every merchant whose `{{available}}` is above 0 exactly that amount. Returns `"merchant_id:amount"` for each payout made, by merchant id ascending (plain string order); merchants with nothing to pay are left out. |
| `{{payout_history}}(day, {{merchant_id}})` | The merchant's payouts, oldest first, as `"day:amount"`. An empty list for an unknown merchant or one never paid. |

**Settlement.** A sale recorded on day `d` settles on day `d + 3`: its net counts toward `{{available}}` from day `d + 3` on. Debits count at once: a refund or a payout reduces `{{available}}` from the day it happens.

**What you already have:**

- `{{balance}}` still counts everything, settled or not, and a payout reduces it by the amount paid.
- `{{activity}}` lists each payout as `"day:payout:-amount"`.
- **Negative balances.** A refund on a sale that was already paid out can take the balance, and `{{available}}`, below 0. Nothing is paid while `{{available}}` is 0 or less: money from later sales first covers the shortfall as it settles.

### Example

```
add_merchant(1, "acme", 290); add_merchant(1, "bolt", 0)
record_sale(1, "acme", 10000)   -> "s1"
record_sale(2, "bolt", 5000)    -> "s2"
available(3, "acme")            -> 0       (s1 settles on day 4)
run_payouts(4)                  -> ["acme:9710"]
refund(6, "s1", 5000)           -> 4855
balance(6, "acme")              -> -4855
run_payouts(7)                  -> ["bolt:5000"]
record_sale(8, "acme", 10000)   -> "s3"
run_payouts(11)                 -> ["acme:4855"]   (s3 settled: 4855 of its 9710 covered the shortfall)
payout_history(12, "acme")      -> ["4:9710", "11:4855"]
```

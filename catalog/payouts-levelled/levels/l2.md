## Level 2: refunds and activity

Buyers get refunds, and merchants want a statement of what moved on their account. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{refund}}(day, {{sale_id}}, amount)` | Gives `amount` of the sale back to the buyer and returns what this takes off the merchant's balance (rule below). Several refunds can hit one sale. Returns `-1`, changing nothing, if the sale doesn't exist, `amount <= 0`, or `amount` is more than what's left of the sale (its amount minus everything refunded so far). |
| `{{activity}}(day, {{merchant_id}})` | Every change to the merchant's balance, oldest first, as `"day:kind:amount"`: `kind` is `sale` (amount is the sale's net, positive) or `refund` (amount is the debit, negative). An empty list for an unknown merchant. |

**The fee follows the refund.** After a refund, the sale's fee is recomputed on what's left of the sale, with the same rounding as Level 1. The platform gives back the difference between the old and the new fee, so the merchant is debited `amount - (old fee - new fee)`. From then on `{{fee}}` returns the new fee. A fully refunded sale has a fee of 0 and has cost the merchant exactly its net.

### Example

```
add_merchant(1, "acme", 290); record_sale(1, "acme", 10000)  -> "s1"
refund(2, "s1", 2500)   -> 2428    (7500 left, fee 290 -> 218)
fee(3, "s1")            -> 218
balance(3, "acme")      -> 7282
refund(4, "s1", 8000)   -> -1      (only 7500 left)
refund(5, "s1", 7500)   -> 7282
activity(7, "acme")     -> ["1:sale:9710", "2:refund:-2428", "5:refund:-7282"]
```

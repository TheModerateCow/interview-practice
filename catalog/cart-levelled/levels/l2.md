## Level 2: checkout and best-sellers

Carts can now be paid for, and merchandising wants to see what sells. Add to `{{Shop}}`:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{checkout}}({{ts}}, {{cart_id}}) → int` | Buys everything in the cart: each product's stock goes down by the line's quantity, the cart is emptied and removed, and the method returns the amount charged (the cart's total). Returns `-1`, changing nothing, if the cart doesn't exist, is empty, or **any** line needs more units than the product has in stock right now (another cart may have bought them first). |
| `{{stock_of}}({{ts}}, {{sku}}) → int` | Units of `{{sku}}` in stock. `-1` for an unknown sku. |
| `{{top_sellers}}({{ts}}, {{n}}) → list of strings` | Up to `{{n}}` products as `"sku(units)"`, where `units` is how many have been bought through successful checkouts: most units first, ties by sku ascending. Products with no units sold are left out. |

After a checkout the cart id can be used again: the next `{{add_to_cart}}` starts a new, empty cart.

### Example

```
{{add_product}}(1, "lamp", 4500, 3); {{add_product}}(2, "bulb", 300, 10)
{{add_to_cart}}(3, "a", "lamp", 2)
{{add_to_cart}}(4, "b", "lamp", 2); {{add_to_cart}}(5, "b", "bulb", 4)
{{checkout}}(6, "a")          → 9000
{{checkout}}(7, "b")          → -1      (1 lamp left)
{{stock_of}}(8, "bulb")        → 10      (nothing was taken)
{{remove_from_cart}}(9, "b", "lamp", 1)
{{checkout}}(10, "b")         → 5700
{{top_sellers}}(11, 5)         → ["bulb(4)", "lamp(3)"]
```

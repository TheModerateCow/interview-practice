## Level 4: history and account merges

Members want their reading history, and the desk keeps finding people with two accounts. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{history}}(ts, member_id) → list of strings` | The title of every successful checkout the member made, oldest first, including loans still out. Repeats stay in. An empty list for a member with none, or an unknown member. |
| `{{merge}}(ts, keep_id, drop_id) → bool` | Folds `{{drop_id}}`'s account into `{{keep_id}}`. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if either member is unknown, they're the same member, both have a copy of the same title on loan, or the merged account would have more than 3 copies on loan. |

After a merge, everything `{{drop_id}}` had belongs to `{{keep_id}}`: its loans (`{{borrower}}` names `{{keep_id}}`; due dates and renewals are unchanged), its history (interleaved with `{{keep_id}}`'s in checkout order) and its fines. A later late return of a moved loan fines `{{keep_id}}`. `{{drop_id}}` no longer exists: every method treats it as unknown, and `{{add_member}}` can register it again as a fresh account. `{{popular}}` counts don't change.

### Example

```
{{add_member}}(1, "ana"); {{add_member}}(2, "ana-old")
{{add_copy}}(3, "D-1", "Dune"); {{add_copy}}(4, "E-1", "Emma"); {{add_copy}}(5, "D-2", "Dune")
{{checkout}}(10, "ana-old", "Dune"); {{return_copy}}(30, "D-1")
{{checkout}}(31, "ana", "Emma"); {{checkout}}(32, "ana-old", "Dune")
{{history}}(33, "ana-old")          → ["Dune", "Dune"]
{{fines}}(34, "ana-old")            → 150
{{merge}}(35, "ana", "ana-old")     → true
{{history}}(36, "ana")              → ["Dune", "Emma", "Dune"]
{{borrower}}(37, "D-1")             → "ana"
{{fines}}(38, "ana")                → 150
{{checkout}}(39, "ana-old", "Dune") → ""     (ana-old no longer exists)
{{merge}}(40, "ana", "ana-old")     → false
```

## Level 2: schedules, rankings and free slots

Reception wants a clinician's day at a glance, a workload ranking, and the next slot they can offer a caller. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{schedule}}({{ts}}, {{clinician}}) → list of strings` | The clinician's appointments that aren't cancelled, as `"id@start-end"`, earliest start first. |
| `{{busiest_clinicians}}({{ts}}, {{n}}) → list of strings` | Up to `{{n}}` clinicians as `"id:minutes"`, most minutes first; ties by id ascending. A clinician's **minutes** are the total length of their appointments that aren't cancelled. Clinicians with nothing booked are included (with `0`) if there's room. |
| `{{next_free}}({{ts}}, {{clinician}}, {{kind}}, {{after}}) → int, or absent` | The earliest start at or after `{{after}}`, and never before `{{ts}}`, at which `{{book}}({{ts}}, {{clinician}}, …, {{kind}}, {{start}})` would succeed: the clinician free and some room free for the type's whole length. Absent if the clinician or type doesn't exist, or the clinic has no rooms. |

Ids sort in plain string order (by character code). Return an empty list when nothing matches (or `{{n}}` is 0).

### Example

```
(dr-lee and dr-ng, one room room-1, types checkup 30 and consult 60)
{{book}}(10, "dr-lee", "pat-1", "checkup", 600)       → "appt1"
{{book}}(11, "dr-ng", "pat-2", "consult", 640)        → "appt2"
{{schedule}}(12, "dr-lee")                            → ["appt1@600-630"]
{{busiest_clinicians}}(13, 5)                          → ["dr-ng:60", "dr-lee:30"]
{{next_free}}(14, "dr-lee", "checkup", 560)            → 560   (560-590 fits before appt1)
{{next_free}}(15, "dr-lee", "checkup", 590)            → 700   (dr-lee is free from 630, but room-1 is busy 640-700)
{{next_free}}(16, "dr-lee", "checkup", 0)              → 16
```

## Level 3: check-ins, no-shows and waitlists

Patients now check in at the front desk, some don't turn up, and the clinic keeps a waitlist per clinician to fill gaps. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{check_in}}({{ts}}, {{id}}) → bool` | Marks a booked appointment's patient as arrived (status `checked-in`). Allowed from 30 minutes before its start; `false`, changing nothing, if it's too early, the id is unknown, or the appointment isn't `booked` (already checked in, cancelled or a no-show). |
| `{{join_waitlist}}({{ts}}, {{patient}}, {{clinician}}, {{kind}}) → bool` | Puts `{{patient}}` at the back of `{{clinician}}`'s waitlist, wanting a `{{kind}}` appointment. `false` if the clinician or type doesn't exist, or the patient is already on that clinician's waitlist. |
| `{{waitlist}}({{ts}}, {{clinician}}) → list of strings` | That clinician's waitlist as `"patient:type"`, front first (an empty list if none). |

**Statuses.** `{{appointment}}` can now also report `checked-in`, `completed` (checked in, and `{{ts}}` is at or after its end) and `no-show`. `{{cancel}}` refuses anything that isn't `booked`.

**No-shows.** A `booked` appointment that nobody checked in becomes a `no-show` at `start + 10`, before anything else that happens at that time: a call at `start + 10` already sees it. The clinic has no clock of its own: no-shows are noticed by the next call of **any** method whose timestamp is at or after that time, and when several are due they're handled in order of that time (ties in booking order). A no-show holds its clinician and room only from `{{start}}` to `start + 10`; the rest of its slot is free. No-shows are left out of `{{schedule}}` and `{{busiest_clinicians}}`.

**Filling gaps from the waitlist.** When a slot is freed, it's offered to the clinician's waitlist, front first: the first waiting patient whose appointment type `{{book}}` would accept starting at the freed time, at the moment the slot was freed, is booked there (normal room rule, next appointment id) and leaves the waitlist. At most one patient is booked per freed slot.

- `{{cancel}}` frees a slot at the cancelled appointment's `{{start}}`, at the time of the call. The new appointment is `booked`.
- A no-show frees a slot at `start + 10`, at that moment (even if it's noticed later). That patient is already waiting in the clinic, so the new appointment is `checked-in` straight away.

### Example

```
(dr-lee, one room room-1, types checkup 30 and consult 60)
{{book}}(10, "dr-lee", "pat-1", "consult", 100)       → "appt1"
{{join_waitlist}}(11, "pat-9", "dr-lee", "checkup")    → true
{{appointment}}(109, "appt1")                          → "dr-lee pat-1 consult room-1 100-160 booked"
{{check_in}}(110, "appt1")                              → false      (a no-show at 110)
{{appointment}}(111, "appt2")                          → "dr-lee pat-9 checkup room-1 110-140 checked-in"
{{waitlist}}(112, "dr-lee")                            → []
{{appointment}}(150, "appt2")                          → "dr-lee pat-9 checkup room-1 110-140 completed"
```

## Level 4: reschedules, hand-overs and history

Patients move appointments, clinicians go on leave and hand their list to a colleague, and the practice manager wants an audit trail. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{reschedule}}({{ts}}, {{id}}, {{new_start}}) → bool` | Moves a `booked` appointment that hasn't started (`{{ts}} < {{start}}`) to `{{new_start}}`, keeping its clinician, patient and type. It needs `{{new_start}} >= {{ts}}`, the clinician free and a room free, ignoring the appointment's own old slot; the room is picked again by the usual rule. `false`, changing nothing, otherwise. The old slot is then offered to the waitlist at its old `{{start}}`, like a cancellation. |
| `{{hand_over}}({{ts}}, {{from_clinician}}, {{to_clinician}}) → list of strings, or absent` | Gives every `booked` appointment of `{{from_clinician}}` to `{{to_clinician}}`, in order of start time. One that would overlap an appointment `{{to_clinician}}` already has (including ones just handed over) is cancelled instead. Returns the cancelled ids in that order (an empty list if none). Appointments keep their rooms and times; ones already checked in, completed, cancelled or no-shows stay with `{{from_clinician}}`. Then `{{from_clinician}}`'s waitlist joins the back of `{{to_clinician}}`'s, in order, skipping patients already waiting for `{{to_clinician}}`, and `{{from_clinician}}`'s waitlist is emptied. `{{from_clinician}}` stays on staff. Returns absent, changing nothing, if either clinician doesn't exist or they're the same. A hand-over never books anyone from a waitlist. |
| `{{history}}({{ts}}, {{id}}) → list of strings` | Everything that happened to the appointment, oldest first, as `"time:event"` (an empty list for an unknown id). |

**History events**, each dated when it happened:

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `booked` | `{{book}}`, or a booking from the waitlist (dated when the slot was freed) |
| `cancelled` | `{{cancel}}`, or a clash in a hand-over |
| `checked-in` | `{{check_in}}`, or a waitlist booking after a no-show (same time as its `booked`) |
| `no-show` | at `start + 10`, even if the clinic only noticed it later |
| `rescheduled:<newStart>` | `{{reschedule}}` |
| `handed-over:<to>` | `{{hand_over}}` |

Reaching `completed` isn't an event.

### Example

```
(dr-lee and dr-ng, one room room-1, types checkup 30 and review 20)
{{book}}(10, "dr-lee", "pat-1", "checkup", 100)       → "appt1"
{{book}}(11, "dr-ng", "pat-2", "review", 300)         → "appt2"
{{reschedule}}(20, "appt1", 290)                      → false      (room-1 is taken at 300)
{{reschedule}}(21, "appt1", 200)                      → true
{{hand_over}}(30, "dr-lee", "dr-ng")                   → []
{{schedule}}(31, "dr-ng")                             → ["appt1@200-230", "appt2@300-320"]
{{history}}(400, "appt1")                             → ["10:booked", "21:rescheduled:200", "30:handed-over:dr-ng", "210:no-show"]
```

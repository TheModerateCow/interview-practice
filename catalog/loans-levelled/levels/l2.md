## Level 2: payments

Borrowers start paying, and sometimes pay more than is due. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{pay}}(day, loan_id, amount)` | Takes a payment and returns the change: the part of `amount` left over once the whole loan is repaid (usually 0). Returns `-1`, changing nothing, if the loan doesn't exist or `amount <= 0`. |
| `{{owed}}(day, loan_id)` | Everything still unpaid on the loan: the interest and principal of every instalment, due or not. `-1` if the loan doesn't exist. |
| `{{principal_left}}(day, loan_id)` | The principal still unpaid, over every instalment. `-1` if the loan doesn't exist. |

**Allocation.** A payment goes to the oldest instalment not paid in full: first its interest, then its principal. Whatever is left moves on to the next instalment, even one that isn't due yet (paying ahead), and so on down the schedule. Past the last instalment, the rest comes back as change.

`{{amount_due}}` now counts only what's still unpaid: a partly paid instalment counts its unpaid part, and an instalment paid ahead may owe nothing when it falls due. `{{schedule}}` doesn't change: it always shows the scheduled amounts.

### Example

```
{{originate}}(1, "car", 90000, 3, 100)   (3 instalments of 30900: interest 900, principal 30000)
{{pay}}(31, "car", 10000)       -> 0
{{principal_left}}(32, "car")   -> 80900    (900 went to interest, 9100 to principal)
{{owed}}(32, "car")             -> 82700
{{pay}}(40, "car", 50000)       -> 0        (20900 clears #1, 29100 goes ahead into #2)
{{amount_due}}(61, "car")       -> 1800
{{pay}}(62, "car", 40000)       -> 7300     (only 32700 was owed)
{{owed}}(63, "car")             -> 0
```

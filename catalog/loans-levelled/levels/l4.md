## Level 4: payoff quotes and restructuring

Borrowers ask what it would cost to close their loan today, and the lender can stretch a loan over more months. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{payoff_quote}}(day, loan_id)` | What it would cost to close the loan today: all the unpaid principal, plus the unpaid interest and fees of instalments due on or before `day`. The interest of instalments not yet due is waived, but interest already paid ahead on them isn't refunded. `0` for a loan with nothing left unpaid, `-1` if it doesn't exist. A quote changes nothing. |
| `{{restructure}}(day, loan_id, months)` | Re-spreads the principal of the instalments not yet due over `months` new instalments. Returns `true` if it did. |

**Restructuring.**

- Instalments due on or before `day` stay as they are, with whatever is still unpaid on them (fees included).
- Every instalment due after `day` is replaced. Their principal is re-spread over `months` new instalments by the Level 1 rules, as if a loan of that principal were originated today at the loan's own `rate_bp`: due on `day + 30 × k`, interest computed on the re-spread principal.
- Returns `false`, changing nothing, if the loan doesn't exist, `months <= 0`, no instalment is due after `day`, or any instalment due after `day` has already been paid toward (paid ahead).
- `{{schedule}}` lists the kept instalments, then the new ones. Payments, fees and status work on the new schedule as before.

### Example

```
{{originate}}(1, "car", 120000, 4, 100)   (4 instalments of 31200, due 31, 61, 91, 121)
{{pay}}(31, "car", 31200)
{{pay}}(50, "car", 1000)               (paid ahead: #2's interest)
{{payoff_quote}}(50, "car")     -> 90000    (#2 isn't due: its interest is waived)
{{restructure}}(50, "car", 3)   -> false    (#2 has been paid toward)
{{payoff_quote}}(61, "car")     -> 90200    (#2's last 200 of interest is now due)
{{restructure}}(62, "car", 3)   -> true     (60000 over 3: interest 600)
{{schedule}}(63, "car")         -> ["31:31200", "61:31200", "92:20600", "122:20600", "152:20600"]
{{amount_due}}(63, "car")       -> 30200
```

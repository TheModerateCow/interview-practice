## Level 3: late fees and loan status

Collections wants a fee on instalments paid late, and a status for every loan. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `{{charge_late_fees}}(day)` | The nightly fee run, over every loan. Charges a late fee of **2500** to each instalment that is past its grace period and not paid in full, unless that instalment was charged a fee before. Returns how many fees it charged. |
| `{{status}}(day, loan_id)` | `"paid_off"` if nothing is left unpaid on the loan, `"late"` if some instalment is past its grace period and not paid in full, otherwise `"current"`. `""` if the loan doesn't exist. |

**Grace period.** An instalment due on day `d` can be paid without a fee through day `d + 10`; from day `d + 11` on it's past its grace period. Fees are only ever charged by `{{charge_late_fees}}`: an instalment paid in full before a run gets no fee, even if it was paid late. Each instalment gets at most one fee.

**New rules for what you already have:**

- A fee belongs to the instalment it was charged to, and is owed with it: `{{amount_due}}` and `{{owed}}` include unpaid fees; `{{principal_left}}` and `{{schedule}}` don't.
- **Allocation** within each instalment becomes fee, then interest, then principal. Payments still go to the oldest instalment not paid in full first.

### Example

```
{{originate}}(1, "car", 60000, 2, 100)   (2 instalments of 30600, due 31 and 61)
{{originate}}(1, "van", 60000, 2, 100)
{{pay}}(31, "van", 30600)
{{pay}}(35, "car", 400)         (interest only: 200 of it still unpaid)
{{charge_late_fees}}(41)        -> 0          (day 41 is car #1's last day of grace)
{{charge_late_fees}}(42)        -> 1          (car #1; van #1 is paid)
{{status}}(42, "car")           -> "late"
{{amount_due}}(42, "car")       -> 32700      (2500 fee + 200 interest + 30000 principal)
{{pay}}(43, "car", 3000)        -> 0          (2500 fee, 200 interest, then 300 principal)
{{principal_left}}(43, "car")   -> 59700
{{charge_late_fees}}(44)        -> 0          (car #1 was charged already)
{{status}}(44, "van")           -> "current"
```

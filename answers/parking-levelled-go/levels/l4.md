## Level 4: history and billing

Support needs to answer "who was in this spot at 9:40?", and billing needs what each plate owes. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `OccupantAt(ts int, spotID string, atTs int) string` | The plate parked in `spotID` after every operation with a timestamp at or before `atTs`, or `""` if the spot was empty then or doesn't exist. |
| `Charges(ts int, plate string) int` | What `plate` owes, in cents, for all its **finished** stays (it has left). `0` for a plate with none. |

A stay runs from the `Park` to the `Leave`. It costs the hourly rate of the **spot's** size for every started hour (60 minutes): `small` 200, `medium` 300, `large` 500 cents. So 60 minutes is 1 hour and 61 minutes is 2. A reservation is never an occupant and is never charged; a stay that started from a reservation is charged from the `Park`.

### Example

```
AddSpot(1, "B1", "medium")
Park(10, "KX-101", "small"); Leave(100, "KX-101")
Park(130, "VN-220", "medium")
OccupantAt(200, "B1", 9)   -> ""
OccupantAt(200, "B1", 99)  -> "KX-101"
OccupantAt(200, "B1", 100) -> ""         (it left at 100)
OccupantAt(200, "B1", 150) -> "VN-220"
Charges(201, "KX-101")     -> 600        (90 minutes in a medium spot: 2 hours x 300)
Charges(202, "VN-220")     -> 0          (still parked)
```

## Level 2: checkout and best-sellers

Carts can now be paid for, and merchandising wants to see what sells. Add to `Shop`:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `long checkout(int ts, String cartId)` | Buys everything in the cart: each product's stock goes down by the line's quantity, the cart is emptied and removed, and the method returns the amount charged (the cart's total). Returns `-1`, changing nothing, if the cart doesn't exist, is empty, or **any** line needs more units than the product has in stock right now (another cart may have bought them first). |
| `int stockOf(int ts, String sku)` | Units of `sku` in stock. `-1` for an unknown sku. |
| `List<String> topSellers(int ts, int n)` | Up to `n` products as `"sku(units)"`, where `units` is how many have been bought through successful checkouts: most units first, ties by sku ascending. Products with no units sold are left out. |

After a checkout the cart id can be used again: the next `addToCart` starts a new, empty cart.

### Example

```
addProduct(1, "lamp", 4500, 3); addProduct(2, "bulb", 300, 10)
addToCart(3, "a", "lamp", 2)
addToCart(4, "b", "lamp", 2); addToCart(5, "b", "bulb", 4)
checkout(6, "a")          -> 9000
checkout(7, "b")          -> -1      (1 lamp left)
stockOf(8, "bulb")        -> 10      (nothing was taken)
removeFromCart(9, "b", "lamp", 1)
checkout(10, "b")         -> 5700
topSellers(11, 5)         -> ["bulb(4)", "lamp(3)"]
```

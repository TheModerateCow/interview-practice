## Level 4: audit history and rollback

A bad launch needs undoing fast, and audit wants to see who changed what when. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `history(ts, flag_id, env) -> list[str]` | Every on/off change of the flag in `env`, oldest first, as `"<time>:on"` or `"<time>:off"`. A change is a successful `set_enabled` (even one that sets the value already there), an applied scheduled change (at its `at_ts`), or a `rollback` (at its `ts`, with the value it restored). Empty for an unknown flag or an environment never changed. |
| `rollback(ts, flag_id, env, to_ts) -> bool` | Restores the flag's on/off state **and** rollout percent in `env` to what they were after every change with a time at or before `to_ts`. Before any change that is off and `100`. The rollback is itself a change made at `ts`: it appears in `history`, and a later rollback to a time at or after `ts` sees what it restored. Targets and pending scheduled changes are untouched. Returns `False` if the flag doesn't exist. |

### Example

```
create_flag(1, "search")
set_enabled(2, "search", "prod", True)
set_enabled(5, "search", "prod", False)
set_enabled(9, "search", "prod", True)
rollback(12, "search", "prod", 5)        -> True    (off, as after time 5)
history(13, "search", "prod")            -> ["2:on", "5:off", "9:on", "12:off"]
```

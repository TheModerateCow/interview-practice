# Phase 3: flash sale

Replenishment is live. Then marketing ran a flash sale. Every product page calls `Available`, every checkout places a `Reserve` (card payments hold stock for 15 minutes; bank transfers and pre-orders hold it for up to 12 hours), and orders ship or get cancelled within seconds. Ops runs `Replenishment` every hour or so and restocks what it suggests. In the first minutes everything is fine. As the sale goes on, every call gets slower, and by the afternoon checkout is timing out.

The workload to handle: 20 SKUs, **80,000 checkouts** a second apart, each with an `Available` check and a `Reserve`, plus the commits, releases, replenishment runs and restocks that go with them. The whole run must take **under 1 second**. Results (what sells out, what ships, what expires, the plans and the balances) must not change.

Find where the time goes and fix it. Bear in mind that holds have different lengths, so a hold placed later can run out sooner, and that tens of thousands of holds are open at the same time.

## Level 4: history and account merges

Members want their reading history, and the desk keeps finding people with two accounts. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `History(ts int, memberID string) []string` | The title of every successful checkout the member made, oldest first, including loans still out. Repeats stay in. An empty list for a member with none, or an unknown member. |
| `Merge(ts int, keepID, dropID string) bool` | Folds `dropID`'s account into `keepID`. Returns `false`, changing nothing, if either member is unknown, they're the same member, both have a copy of the same title on loan, or the merged account would have more than `MaxLoans` copies on loan. |

After a merge, everything `dropID` had belongs to `keepID`: its loans (`Borrower` names `keepID`; due dates and renewals are unchanged), its history (interleaved with `keepID`'s in checkout order) and its fines. A later late return of a moved loan fines `keepID`. `dropID` no longer exists: every method treats it as unknown, and `AddMember` can register it again as a fresh account. `Popular` counts don't change.

### Example

```
AddMember(1, "ana"); AddMember(2, "ana-old")
AddCopy(3, "D-1", "Dune"); AddCopy(4, "E-1", "Emma"); AddCopy(5, "D-2", "Dune")
Checkout(10, "ana-old", "Dune"); Return(30, "D-1")
Checkout(31, "ana", "Emma"); Checkout(32, "ana-old", "Dune")
History(33, "ana-old")          -> ["Dune", "Dune"]
Fines(34, "ana-old")            -> 150
Merge(35, "ana", "ana-old")     -> true
History(36, "ana")              -> ["Dune", "Emma", "Dune"]
Borrower(37, "D-1")             -> "ana"
Fines(38, "ana")                -> 150
Checkout(39, "ana-old", "Dune") -> ""     (ana-old no longer exists)
Merge(40, "ana", "ana-old")     -> false
```

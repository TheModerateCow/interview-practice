## Level 4: rate history

Revenue management changes room rates, and accounting needs to know what a room cost on any past day. Add:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `set_rate(day, room_id: str, rate: int) -> bool` | From now on the room costs `rate` per night. Returns `False`, changing nothing, if the room doesn't exist or `rate <= 0`. |
| `rate_at(day, room_id: str, at_day: int) -> int` | The room's nightly rate after every change made on or before `at_day` (several changes on the same day: the last one counts). `-1` if the room doesn't exist, or `at_day` is before the day the room was added. |

**Bookings keep their price.** A booking is charged at the rate the room had when it was booked, whatever `set_rate` does later: this applies to `cost` and `check_out`. `available_rooms` sorts by the current rate.

### Example

```
add_room(1, "101", "double", 12000)
book(2, "maya", "101", 10, 13)  -> "b1"
set_rate(5, "101", 15000)       -> True
cost(6, "b1")                   -> 36000   (booked at 12000)
rate_at(6, "101", 4)            -> 12000
rate_at(6, "101", 5)            -> 15000
rate_at(6, "101", 0)            -> -1
```
